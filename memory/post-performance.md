## Patterns

### What's Working
1. **Aliyah JournalLock is now #1** — 1,522 avg views/reel, overtaking ManifestLock (1,078). The journaling angle has more emotional resonance than manifestation.
2. **Question hooks outperform everything** — "wait why does..." format averages 1,316 views. Creates instant curiosity.
3. **Plant/guilt metaphor hooks remain top tier** — avg 1,194 views. The unexpected metaphor drives comments and shares.
4. **Phone personification still strong** — avg 1,177 views. "my phone has opinions now" type hooks.
5. **Combining two patterns is the multiplier** — Question + phone personification (2,069 views), Math + social context (3,057 views).
6. **Emilly broke through** — avg went from 150-200 to 906 views/reel. The "127 pickups" and "11 years" hooks unlocked this account.
7. **Specific numbers + social context = viral** — the #1 reel (3,057 views) combines therapist context + caught-in-the-act moment.

### What's NOT Working
1. **"i did the math" without a twist** — 71 reels use this pattern. When it's just "i did the math on my screen time... [big number]" it averages 150-250 views. It ONLY works when paired with contrast ("2,847 vs 3"), social context ("in front of her"), or unexpected framing ("200+ pickups was my wake up call lol").
2. **Sophie.unplugs** — 491 avg views, weakest by far. Even the same hooks that get 1,000+ on other accounts get <200 here. The account itself is the problem.
3. **Confession hooks without surprise** — "honestly wasn't going to post this" averages 461 views. The vulnerability frame only works if the reveal is genuinely surprising.
4. **Repetitive hooks across accounts** — many reels share nearly identical captions. Instagram's algorithm likely penalizes near-duplicate content across accounts posting from the same network.

### Audience Signals
- **Aliyah accounts still get "what app is this?" comments** — highest-intent signal
- **Sanya's therapy-context reels drive comments asking about the phone feature** — the "caught in session" framing generates curiosity
- **Plant metaphor reels get saves** — the emotional hook makes people bookmark for later
