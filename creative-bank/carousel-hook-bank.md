# Carousel Hook Bank — JournalLock brand page

> **INSTRUCTIONS FOR CLAUDE**: Pick hooks from this bank or riff close variations.
> Rotation rule: no hook pattern repeat within 14 days on the brand page.
> Every carousel = HOOK slide → prompt/value slides (the sendable payload) → CTA
> slide (save + follow + one-line app mention). Design for SENDS first, saves
> second — sends are IG's #1 ranking signal (2026), and prompt lists are what
> get forwarded (alena: 80.9K sends vs 69.3K likes).

Sources: teardown of 6 viral journaling carousels (2026-07-14, screenshots in
chat; biggest = alenaarosalie Notes-screenshot format) + verified deep-research
(Mosseri statements, Buffer 4M-post analysis). Template families in
`scripts/carousel.py`: T1 notes / T2 card / T3 photo.

## Pattern 1: Blank-Page Paralysis (THE niche winner — 3 of 6 references)

The audience's own words. Agitate the staring-at-a-blank-page moment, promise
instant relief. Best on T1 (Notes) and T3.

1. "i want to journal but i never know what to write."
2. "journal ideas for when you don't know what to write"
3. "journal prompts for when your mind won't quiet down"
4. "doesn't it suck when you're staring at your journal and your mind is blank"
5. "for everyone who buys journals and never fills them"
6. "your journal isn't empty because you're lazy. you just need a prompt."

## Pattern 2: Time-of-Day / Ritual Menus (alexa 10K sends, diaryoncam 5.7K)

Morning/night prompt menus; sunday reset; monthly reset. Naturally habitual →
follows. Best on T3 (photo card) and T1.

7. "morning prompts ☀️ / evening prompts 🌙" (two-menu slide)
8. "sunday reset journal ideas"
9. "new month, new pages — august reset prompts"
10. "5-minute night routine for your brain"

## Pattern 3: Audience Callouts (identity hook)

Name the exact person. They tag/send themselves.

11. "journal prompts for overthinkers"
12. "journal prompts for people who think journaling is cringe"
13. "for the 2am overthinkers"
14. "journal prompts if you can't say it out loud yet"
15. "prompts for the friend who's 'fine'"

## Pattern 4: Themed Prompt Packs (the save/send payload)

Emotional themes, one per slide: brain dump · self-love · gratitude · closure ·
who am i · anxiety · glow-up · self-care. (alena's slides were exactly these.)

16. "journal prompts — who am i 💌"
17. "brain dump prompts for a loud head"
18. "self-love prompts you'll actually answer"
19. "closure prompts for the chapter you're done with"
20. "gratitude prompts that aren't cheesy"

## Pattern 5: Setup / Guide (euyos 13.1K — the follow driver)

Editorial how-to; positions the page as the journaling authority. Best on T2.

21. "how i set up my 2026 journal"
22. "starting a journal you'll actually keep — a gentle guide"
23. "journal guide for beginners (save for later)"
24. "the 3-minute journaling method for busy people"

## Pattern 6: Signs / Symptoms (curiosity + self-diagnosis)

25. "signs your brain needs a journal, not another scroll"
26. "5 signs you need a brain dump tonight"
27. "signs journaling would actually help you (from someone who resisted it)"

## Pattern 7: Series / Part-2 Mechanic (alena's "part 2" — follow driver)

Number everything. "Part 2", "vol. 3", "prompt menu #12" — trains the follow.

28. "you liked part 1, so here's part 2 — happy writing 🤍"
29. "prompt menu no. 2 — for heavier weeks"
30. "30 days of prompts, day 1" (daily series anchor)
