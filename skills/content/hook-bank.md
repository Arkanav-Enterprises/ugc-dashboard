# Hook Bank

> **INSTRUCTIONS FOR CLAUDE**: Pick hooks from this bank or create close variations.
> Never repeat the same hook on the same account within 14 days.
> Never use the same hook on two accounts the same day.
> Always match the hook to the persona's voice (check persona files).

## Pattern 1: Phone Personification (Best Performer)

Structure: "my phone [unexpected human behavior]"
Why it works: Phone-as-character creates curiosity. Feels relatable, not promotional.

### ManifestLock variants
1. "my phone won't unlock until i read my affirmations out loud"
2. "my phone locks itself every morning until i manifest"
3. "my phone won't let me scroll until i say nice things about myself"
4. "my phone is more committed to my goals than i am"
5. "my phone started holding me accountable and honestly it's working"
6. "my phone gives me the silent treatment until i do my affirmations"

### JournalLock variants
7. "my phone won't unlock until i trauma dump"
8. "my phone guilt trips me with a tiny plant now"
9. "my phone guilt trips me with a dying plant now"
10. "my phone won't let me doom scroll until i journal"
11. "my phone locked me out until i wrote about my feelings"
12. "my phone decided i need therapy and it's not wrong"

## DEAD HOOKS — Never Use These

These patterns consistently get 100-130 views. Do not generate anything resembling them:

- "my phone won't let me scroll anymore" (too vague, no stakes)
- "my phone won't let me be toxic anymore" (vague, no specifics)
- "this app changed my life" (banned phrase, overclaim)
- "check out this app that blocks your phone" (promotional, algorithm suppresses)
- Any hook mentioning "Manifest Lock" or "Journal Lock" by name
- Any hook referencing outdoor settings or studio lighting
- Generic "i'm manifesting" or "i started journaling" without conflict or surprise
