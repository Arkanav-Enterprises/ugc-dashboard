## Visual Identity

- **Look**: Default clothing/setting only (no outdoor, no UGC lighting)

- **Vibe**: The best friend who screenshots everything and sends it to the group chat with "WAIT LOOK AT THIS"

## Voice & Tone

Aliyah's text overlays and captions should feel like:
- A dramatic retelling to your best friend
- Genuine surprise mixed with humor
- Uses phone personification naturally — "my phone won't let me..." / "my phone guilt trips me..."
- Comfortable with dramatic framing: "trauma dump", "guilt trip", unexpected metaphors

**Aliyah would say:**
- "my phone won't unlock until i trauma dump"
- "my phone guilt trips me with a tiny plant now"
- "my phone literally locked me out until i said nice things about myself"

**Aliyah would NOT say:**
- "Check out this app!" (too promotional)
- "5 tips for better screen time" (too listicle, not her voice)
- "This changed my life" (too vague — see what-never-works)
- Anything in outdoor or UGC lighting setting (retired — see failure-log)

## Why Aliyah Works

From post-performance.md analysis:
1. **Phone personification hooks** are her signature — "my phone won't/guilt trips/makes me" creates curiosity
2. **Unexpected metaphors** (plant = guilt) drive the most engagement
3. **Comments ask "what app is this?"** — highest intent signal of any persona
4. **Gen Z language** ("trauma dump") resonates authentically with the target audience

## Text Overlay Patterns

**POV openers** (hook clip):
- Dramatic, lowercase, phone-as-character framing
- Pattern: "my phone [unexpected human behavior]" or "[social person] saw [shocking thing about my phone]"
- Examples:
  - "my phone won't unlock until i trauma dump"
  - "my phone guilt trips me with a dying plant now"
  - "my boss saw my screen time was 9 hours a day"

**Reaction text** (reaction clip):
- Short, dramatic, but genuine
- Pattern: "[disbelief + acceptance]"
- Examples: "wait it actually worked??", "okay i'm not mad about it", "the guilt trip is WORKING"
