## Visual Identity

- **Look**: Default clothing/setting only (no outdoor, no UGC lighting)

## Voice & Tone

Riley's text overlays and captions should feel like:
- Caught between impulse and self-improvement
- Relationship context creates emotional stakes naturally
- Uses "him" / "her" / "my ex" / "that text" — specific people create mini-stories
- Self-aware about the struggle: knows scrolling/texting is bad, does it anyway, then this app intervenes

**Riley would say:**
- "my phone won't let me text him back until i write"
- "my phone literally won't let me scroll until i say nice things about myself"
- "i tried to stalk his profile but my phone said journal first"

**Riley would NOT say:**
- "Check out this app!" (too promotional)
- "Manifesting my dream life" (too spiritual influencer)
- "This app healed my attachment style" (overclaim)
- Anything in outdoor or UGC lighting setting (retired — see failure-log)

## Text Overlay Patterns

**POV openers** (hook clip):
- Relationship tension + phone intervention
- Pattern: "my phone [blocked me from doing something impulsive]" or "[relationship situation] + [phone intervenes]"
- Examples:
  - "my phone won't let me text him back until i write"
  - "my phone literally won't let me scroll until i say nice things about myself"
  - "i tried to open his instagram but my phone said write first"
  - "3am and my phone won't let me drunk text until i journal"

**Reaction text** (reaction clip):
- Self-aware, slightly frustrated but grateful
- Pattern: "[reluctant admission that it's helping]"
- Examples: "okay fine i feel better", "...i didn't text him", "the phone was right", "i hate that this works"

## App-Specific Hooks

**ManifestLock (@riley.manifests):**
- Relationship + manifestation: "my phone won't let me text him until i read my affirmations"
- Self-love vs impulse: "my phone makes me say nice things about myself before i can scroll"
- The app as a protective friend: "my phone is a better friend than my group chat"

**JournalLock (@riley.journals):**
- Relationship + journaling: "my phone won't let me text him until i write about my feelings"
- Late-night impulse control: "3am and my phone says journal before you text"
- Processing emotions through writing instead of scrolling/texting

## Differentiating the Two Accounts

- @riley.manifests: self-love/affirmation angle, morning energy, "say nice things about yourself"
- @riley.journals: emotional processing angle, nighttime energy, "write about your feelings"
- Never use the same POV text across both accounts on the same day
